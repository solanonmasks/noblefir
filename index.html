Nine steps, no guesswork

You'll always know what's happening this week and what's next. Pick a stage to see exactly what we do.

01

Thorough consultation

Free, on-site, no pressure. We listen to your goals, your design ideas and your budget before we say a word about cost.

02

Transparent quote

A clear, itemised estimate with every service cost stated upfront. Honest pricing, no hidden fees, no surprises later.

03

Material selection

Trade access to our partner suppliers — premium fixtures and finishes, chosen with guidance instead of guesswork.

04

Preparation & permits

We pull every permit and licence and manage strata approvals, so the build starts on time and stays legal.

05

Professional execution

Our own crews, dust control daily, and a weekly progress update so you're never wondering where things stand.

Checks at every critical stage — framing, rough-in, waterproofing — signed off before anything gets closed up.

We review every element together. The project isn't finished until you say it is — 100% satisfied, in writing.

Post-project support
